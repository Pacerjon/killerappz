Getting started

Listworthy has five tabs: Lists, Discover, Search, Rank, and You. If you're new, start in Discover and tap any of the 130+ ready-made lists, or tap the + button on the Lists tab to build your own. Choose a category (Movies, TV, Music, Books, Games, Places, Products, Checklists, and more), give it a title, and start adding items.

List types

Every list can be shown three ways. Switch with the buttons at the top of the list.

  • Checklist: check things off as you go. Great for shows to watch, books to read, and places to visit.
  • Ranked: your list sorted best to worst, with your top 3 on a podium.
  • Tiers: sort items into S, A, B, C, D, and F.

Head-to-head ranking

In a ranked list, new items wait for a rank. Tap Rank new on the list (or open the Rank tab) and pick the better of two titles. Listworthy places each item in the right spot, usually in a few taps, and saves its score as you go, so you can stop any time.

  • Too close to call ties the two items.
  • Haven't seen it skips an item and leaves it unranked.
  • You can still open any item and set its 1 to 10 score by hand.

List & item actions

  • Press and hold a list (or swipe left in list view) to Rename, Change Icon, or Delete. Ranked lists also offer Rank head-to-head.
  • Swipe left on an item to Rename, Move, or Delete.
  • Swipe right on an item to set a reminder.
  • Search inside a list to find titles in that list's category and add them straight to it.

Sharing from other apps

Listworthy supports the iOS Share Sheet. From apps like Safari, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, or Maps, choose Share → Listworthy to instantly create a new item or list with the shared link, title, and image filled in automatically. You can also paste a link directly into any Links, Products, or Miscellaneous list.

Sharing, exporting & importing

  • Story card: In a ranked list, tap Story card to share your top 5 as an image sized for Instagram and TikTok stories. It includes a QR code so friends can get Listworthy.
  • Share as image: Open a list → Share → Share as Image to post your whole list as a graphic.
  • Export as file: Tap Share → Export to save a .listworthy file with all your list data (Silver and Gold).
  • Import: Tap a shared .listworthy file in any app, then choose “Open in Listworthy” (Silver and Gold).

Reminders

Swipe right on an item and tap Remind to pick a date and time. The first time you set a reminder, iOS asks permission to send notifications. If you said no, turn them on in Settings → Listworthy → Notifications. Reminders are local, no sign-in required.

Widgets

Press and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Listworthy. Choose My List to show one of your lists, or Today's Deal. Press and hold a My List widget and tap Edit Widget to pick which list it shows.

Location

Listworthy only asks for your location when you search Places, so results start near you. You can choose Approximate or Precise, or say no. Change it any time in Settings → Listworthy → Location.

Shopping links

On product items, Listworthy shows direct buy buttons for both Amazon and eBay, and shared product lists include a QR code so friends can jump straight to the item.

iCloud sync & supporter tiers

iCloud sync is free for everyone and keeps your lists backed up across your Apple devices automatically. Optional one-time supporter tiers help fund development: Silver unlocks advanced export and import, and Gold includes everything in Silver plus hides the daily deal card. Listworthy has no ads.
