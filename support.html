Getting started

Listworthy has five tabs: Lists, Discover, Search, Rank, and You. New here? The Lists tab gets you going in one tap: rank your top 10 movies, start a watchlist, or make a wishlist. Or open Discover and start from a ready-made list, or tap + to build your own. Pick a category (Movies, TV, Music, Books, Games, Comics, Places, Products, Links, and more), name it, and start adding.

List types

Every list can be shown three ways. Switch with the selector at the top of the list.

  • Checklist: check things off as you go. Great for shows to watch, books to read, and places to visit.
  • Ranked: your list sorted best to worst, with your top 3 on a podium.
  • Tiers: sort items into S, A, B, C, D, and F.

On the Lists tab, sort your lists (your own order, A to Z, last changed, or newest) and switch between grid and list view.

Saving anything

  • Movies, shows, books, games, music, comics: search and add with posters, years, and details. Keep going suggests what fits the list next, like the rest of a franchise or more from the same author or artist. Tap a suggestion to see what it is.
  • Recipes: save from almost any recipe site and get just the ingredients and steps. Mark it Made it and give it a 1 to 10 score, then filter to the ones you've made.
  • Gift lists: track who each gift is for, what it costs, and what you've bought, against a budget you set.
  • Saved links: paste or share a link, TikTok, or video and it comes back with a title and thumbnail.
  • Places: restaurants and spots save at the exact location you picked, with a map.

Games

Open the Rank tab, pick a category, then pick a game. Today's Pick up top has a new game and category every day, and seasonal packs (Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and more) show up when they're in season.

  • Quick Rank: 10 quick head-to-head picks build your all-time top 10. Keep going adds 10 more. The first time you play a category, Listworthy asks before saving it as a list; after that, it saves as you play.
  • Higher or Lower: does the next title score higher or lower? Albums and comics play by release year. One miss ends the run.
  • Blind Ranking: titles show up one at a time and each goes in a spot from 1 to 10 for good.
  • Brackets: 8 or 16 titles, round by round, until one is champion. Play a category or turn any of your lists into a bracket.
  • Desert Island: you can only keep 5. Choose wisely.
  • Budget Draft: $150 to draft the best 5. Titles are priced $10 to $50 by their score, so the best ones cost the most. Keep $10 for each pick you still have to make. The finish screen shows how close you came to the best team money could buy.
  • Best of the year: in December, rank the year's best and see how your picks line up with the critics'.

Every game can save a real list you can keep, share, and argue about.

Head-to-head ranking

In a ranked list, new items wait for a rank. Tap Rank new on the list (or Rank head-to-head from the list's menu) and pick the better of two titles. Listworthy places each item in the right spot, usually in a few taps, and saves as you go, so you can stop any time.

  • Too close to call ties the two items.
  • Haven't seen it sits under each title, so you can skip either one.
  • You can still open any item and set its 1 to 10 score by hand.

List & item actions

  • Press and hold a list (or swipe left in list view) to Rename, Change Icon, or Delete. Ranked lists also offer Rank head-to-head.
  • Swipe left on an item to Rename, Move, or Delete.
  • Swipe right on an item to set a reminder.
  • Search inside a list to find titles in that list's category and add them straight to it.

Adding items

In a list, tap Add to [list name], then Search to look it up with posters and details, Paste a link to add something you copied (the paste button lights up when a link is ready), or Type your own to add it exactly as typed.

Start with scores: when you create a list from a movie, TV, game, or book template, turn on Start with … scores to begin with TMDB, IGDB, or Open Library scores. Tier templates can sort into S through F the same way. Change any score you disagree with.

Sharing from other apps

From apps like Safari, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, or Maps, choose Share → Listworthy to add the link, title, and image to a list. You can also paste a link straight into any list.

Sharing your lists

  • Story card: in a ranked list, tap Share → Story Card to share your top 5 as an image sized for Instagram and TikTok stories, with a QR code so friends can get Listworthy. Every game's finish screen has one too.
  • Share as image: open a list → Share → List Image to post your whole list (or your tier list) as a graphic.
  • Send a Copy: open a list → Share → Send a Copy. The other person taps the link, then Open in Listworthy, and the whole list is added to their lists with posters, scores, tiers, and notes. It's their own copy, so changes on either phone stay separate. Very long lists go as a .listworthy file instead. They need Listworthy 5.1 or later.
  • Got a .listworthy file? Tap it in Messages, Mail, or Files and choose Listworthy, or go to You → Settings & About → Import List.

Reminders

Swipe right on an item and tap Remind. Pick In 1 hour, Tonight, Tomorrow, or This weekend, or choose a date and time, and add a note if you like.

The reminder shows the poster and says what to do ("Watch: The Odyssey"). Press and hold it for Done (checks the item off), Remind me in 1 hour, or Remind me tomorrow. A red bell on an item means its reminder has passed.

The first time you set a reminder, iOS asks permission to send notifications. If you said no, turn them on in Settings → Listworthy → Notifications. Reminders stay on your phone, no sign-in required.

Widgets

Press and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Listworthy. Choose My List to show one of your lists, or Today's Deal. Press and hold a My List widget and tap Edit Widget to pick which list it shows.

Location

Listworthy only asks for your location when you search Places, so results start near you. You can choose Approximate or Precise, or say no. Change it any time in Settings → Listworthy → Location.

Deals & shopping links

The Lists tab has a daily deal, and Search has weekly deals by category. Product items show buy buttons for Amazon and eBay, and shared product lists include a QR code so friends can jump straight to the item.

Store links in Listworthy are affiliate links: if you buy something, Listworthy may earn a small commission at no cost to you. That's how the app stays free.

iCloud sync & tips

Everything in Listworthy is free, including iCloud sync, which keeps your lists backed up across your Apple devices automatically. No ad banners, no account. If you want to chip in, there's an optional Tip Jar under You → Settings & About. Tips are one-time and unlock nothing extra.

Contact
