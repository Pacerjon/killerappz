What you can do

One app for every list

Ranked lists to settle the debate, tier lists to start one, checklists to get things done, and a fast way to capture anything worth remembering.

⚡

Quick Rank

A 10-pick game that builds your all-time rankings, now with Food & Drink and seasonal packs like Thanksgiving Movies and Christmas Songs.

🏝️

Desert Island

Stranded with room for only 5. Pick from 15 titles in any category or from your own list, then share your island.

🍟

Food & Drink

Rank fast food, candy, cereal, and soda, plus the throwbacks you grew up on.

🙈

Blind Ranking

Ten titles, one at a time. Lock each one into a slot from 1 to 10 before you see what's next.

🏟️

Brackets

8 or 16 picks from any category or your own list, head to head until a champion is crowned.

🎁

Gift lists

Track people, prices, and a budget, with product photos and buy links.

🍳

Recipe cards

Save recipes from almost any site or Pinterest and get clean ingredients and steps. Then rank the ones you've made.

🥊

Rank head-to-head

Not sure if it's an 8 or a 9? Just pick between two. Listworthy places each new item in the right spot, usually in a few taps.

👑

Crown your #1

Your top 3 land on a podium at the top of every ranked list, and #1 gets the crown.

🏆

Tier lists

Sort anything into S, A, B, C, D, and F. Perfect for games, franchises, snacks, or anything worth arguing about.

📲

Story cards

Turn your top 5 into a card sized for Instagram and TikTok stories, or share any list as a polished image with a QR code.

✨

Discover what's hot

See what everyone is watching and playing this week, and start from 150 ready-made lists.

Save links from anywhere

Know where to stream

Reminders that stick
