Privacy Policy

Listworthy is built privacy-first. No accounts, we never see your lists, and they stay in your own iCloud. Starting with version 3.3, Listworthy has no banner ads and no ad tracking.

Effective: October 21, 2026

Summary

  • Your lists, your iCloud. List data is stored in your personal iCloud via Apple CloudKit. We do not run accounts or store your lists on our servers.
  • Location (Approximate or Precise, your choice) is used only to power the in-app Places search. We do not store or sell your location data.
  • Search: when you search, your search terms are sent to the content and map services that supply results. We don't store your searches or link them to you.
  • No ad tracking: starting with version 3.3, Listworthy contains no advertising SDKs and does not track you. Earlier versions showed Google AdMob banner ads (details below).
  • Daily deals: the app downloads a list of hand-picked deals from this website. That download carries no personal information, and deal links are affiliate links.
  • Analytics & diagnostics: we use standard platform diagnostics (such as Apple crash logs) to improve stability. No third-party analytics SDKs collect your list content.

Lists & iCloud sync

Your lists and item data are stored in your iCloud account using CloudKit, which enables seamless syncing across your Apple devices. iCloud sync is free for all users. We cannot access your iCloud. Deleting the app may remove local data; iCloud copies persist until removed from iCloud.

Location

Listworthy requests Location permission only if you use Places search. You may choose Approximate or Precise location and can change this anytime in iOS Settings. Location is used to query Apple's map services for nearby results and is not retained by us.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Listworthy may send local notifications at times you schedule. You can disable notifications at any time in Settings.

Saved links

When you save a link from another app, Listworthy sends that web address to our link-preview service so it can fetch the page's title and preview image. The address is used only to answer that request and is not stored or linked to you. For some sites, the preview image may load from the third-party icon service unavatar.io, which receives the site's domain name.

Daily deals & shopping links

Listworthy shows a hand-picked daily deal and category deals. The app downloads the current deals list from killerappz.dev; that request includes no personal information, and like any web request, the server sees your IP address only to deliver it. Product images load from Amazon and eBay, which may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when they load.

Deals and product items include affiliate links to Amazon and eBay. Tapping one opens that retailer in your browser or app. If you buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These links never change the price you pay, and we do not share your information with retailers. Anything you do on a retailer's site is handled under that retailer's privacy policy.

Advertising (versions before 3.3)

Listworthy version 3.3 and later contain no advertising SDKs and show no banner ads. Earlier versions of the free app used Google AdMob to display banner ads. If you are still running an earlier version, AdMob may collect information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we don't receive or store it. If iOS asks whether to allow tracking, you can decline and still use every feature. Updating to the latest version removes AdMob entirely. For authorized sellers, see /app-ads.txt on this domain.

This website

killerappz.dev uses no cookies, accounts, or analytics. Our Recommends and Deals pages contain Amazon affiliate links: if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Product images on those pages load from Amazon's servers, so Amazon may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when they load, and Amazon handles anything you do on its site under its own privacy policy.

Your controls

  • Location: grant, deny, or switch Approximate/Precise in iOS Settings > Privacy > Location Services.
  • Notifications: enable or disable in Settings > Notifications.
  • Lists: delete data locally and/or from iCloud via iOS Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email listworthy_support@icloud.com.
