# Listworthy app posts: playbook

Fun posts about the app, 3 a week (Tue, Thu, Sat), posted as a photo to the
Listworthy App Facebook page by Make (scenario "Listworthy app posts", reads
https://killerappz.dev/app/posts.xml at noon Eastern) and pinned to the
Listworthy App Pinterest board (Pinterest reads https://killerappz.dev/app/pins.xml,
tall images from app/posts/pins/). Each card slug has both a wide and a tall image. Deals posts are a
separate pipeline; never mix them.

## Voice
- Plain, friendly, a little playful. Talk like a person who loves lists, not a brand.
- No em dashes. No buzzwords. No "game-changer", "unlock", "elevate", "seamless".
- 2 to 6 short lines, under 500 characters. One or two emoji at most.
- End with a question or a nudge to comment when the format allows it.
- No URL in the caption; the build script adds the App Store link.
- At most 2 hashtags, only if natural (#Listworthy plus one topical tag).
- Never claim features the app doesn't have. Use only the facts below.

## Formats (rotate; don't use the same format twice in a row)
1. This or that: two well-known titles in a seasonal pack or category. "Which one wins? Comment below."
2. Rank these 5: five well-known titles; ask people to post their order in the comments.
3. How to, in 3 steps: one feature, exact taps from the facts below.
4. Feature spotlight: what it is, why it's fun, one line on how to start.
5. Seasonal tie-in: a Quick Rank pack or template that is live right now (check app/content.json for packs whose start/end includes today).
6. Hidden tip: a small thing people miss (Keep going, skips in Blind Ranking, long-press menu, Haven't seen it, story cards, the shuffle button on Today's Pick, New island).

## Cards (image slugs in app/posts/cards/)
blind-ranking, brackets, quick-rank, head-to-head, podium, tier-list,
story-card, templates, discover, gift-list, recipe-card, recipes, recipe-duel,
desert-island, todays-pick.
Pick the card that matches the post. Don't reuse a card used in the last 3 posts.

## Facts (only claim these)
- Free on iPhone and iPad. Everything is free as of 5.0: no paid tiers, no ads, iCloud sync included. Optional Tip Jar (unlocks nothing). Never mention Silver, Gold or Bronze.
- Categories: movies, TV, music, books, games, comics, places, products, links.
- Version 5.0 (Nov 2026) made everything free, added the Tip Jar and the smart paste button, plus Fall Movies and Hallmark Christmas Movies packs.
- Version 4.8 (Nov 2026) added Desert Island, Food & Drink in Quick Rank, Today's Pick and trending albums and books.
- Rank tab: Today's Pick at the top suggests a game and category each day (shuffle for another). Below it, "Or pick your own": tap a category tile, then a game tile.
- Rank categories: Movies, TV, Games, Books, Albums, Comics, Food & Drink, plus seasonal packs while they're live.
- Food & Drink: fast food, snacks, candy, cereal and soda, plus throwbacks like Dunkaroos, Gushers, Surge, Crystal Pepsi, French Toast Crunch, Oreo O's, Push Pops and Choco Taco. Plays in every Rank game. Skip with "Never tried it".
- Desert Island: you're stranded and can bring only 5. 15 titles are dealt; tap to pack 5, tap again to leave one behind, then "Lock in my 5". Share your island as a story card or save it as a list. "New island" deals a fresh 15. Works on any category, or long-press a list with 8+ items and choose "Desert Island: keep 5".
- Quick Rank: two posters, tap the better one. A round is 10 picks and builds your all-time top 10. "Keep going" plays more. "Haven't seen it" adds the title to a watchlist. Share your top 5 as a story card at the end.
- Seasonal Quick Rank packs come and go by date (see content.json), e.g. Thanksgiving Movies (Nov 3 to 28), Christmas Movies, Christmas Songs, Holiday TV Specials and Holiday Books (Nov 15 to Dec 31).
- Blind Ranking: 10 titles show up one at a time; put each in a slot from 1 to 10 before you see the next. No take-backs. 3 skips for ones you don't know.
- Brackets: 8 or 16 picks go head to head until one champion is left. Start from the Rank tab (tap 8 or 16 on the Brackets tile), or long-press any list with 8 or more items and tap "Make a bracket". Share the finished bracket as an image.
- Head-to-head ranking: in a ranked list, pick between two and Listworthy places each item in the right spot. Long-press a list for "Rank head-to-head".
- Podium: your top 3 sit on a podium; #1 gets the crown.
- Tier lists: sort anything into S, A, B, C, D, F. New tier templates: fast food chains, Halloween candy, breakfast cereals, pizza toppings, sodas.
- Templates: tap + on the Lists tab. Search 150 templates, filter by Ranked, Tier, Checklist, Recipes or Gifts. "Seasonal Lists" row up top.
- Gift lists: track who gets what, prices, what's bought and a budget. Add gifts by pasting a link or sharing from Amazon or another store app.
- Recipe cards: share a recipe page or a Pinterest pin to Listworthy and get clean ingredients and steps. Cook mode keeps the screen on. Tap "I made this" and give it a score.
- Which was better?: once 2 or more recipes are marked made, open the Made it filter and tap "Which was better?" to rank them head to head.
- Story cards: share your top 5 as a card sized for Instagram and TikTok stories.
- Save links from anywhere: tap Share in TikTok, YouTube, Safari, Reddit or X and send it to a list.
- Discover: what's trending this week in movies, shows, games, albums (Apple Music charts) and books (Apple Books charts), the Seasonal Quick Rank row, plus ready-made lists.
- Widgets for your lists and Today's Deal; streaks and stats.
- Sort your lists: Custom, A to Z, Recent or Newest.
